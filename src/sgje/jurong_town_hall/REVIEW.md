# Jurong Town Hall

Top reference also informed a 14 m-wide recessed central roof court with a
rectangular skylight and bridge service structures. Street reference is dominated
by foreground tree photogrammetry and cannot validate facade details.

Original procedural geometry anchored on OSM way **96764520**, using its exact
upper perimeter split into the two unequal chamfered hulls. The smaller lower
plates, splayed upper walls, recessed ribbon windows, sloping brise-soleil,
pilotis, glazed central concourse, roof seams, hoods, stairs and periscope clock
terminal are hand modelled from the supplied photographs and image references.
There are no logos, text, photograph textures or external meshes.

## Dimensions and references

Current main parapet **26 m**, ventilation hoods **30.69 m**, clock maximum
**59.3 m**. Top and south images locate the clock at approximately local east +8 m, south
+29 m (long-axis u +23 m, transverse v +20 m), corrected after the first
matched-camera render revealed an initial misplaced mast.

Clock maximum uses `/workspace/je_refs/jurong_town_hall/meta.json` measured
59.3 m. Its p90 roof is 30.7 m and p50 roof 26 m, consistent with the
26 m parapet and 30.69 m ventilation hoods. NHB's Jurong Heritage Trail independently quotes
a 58 m clock tower:
https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/Places/landmarks/jurong-heritage-trail/former-jurong-town-hall
Other authoritative descriptions give 50 m (and Docomomo 46 m), so the tile
measurement is preferred. The owned module registers 59.3 m locally, so its card, runtime stats and night
shader height agree with the geometry. The shared catalogue remains untouched
and should be regenerated by the lead as planned.

Daytime details checked against the four supplied Commons photographs, especially
Smuconlaw's 20150611-01 (two hull ends), 20150611-04 (cantilevers and columns),
20150611-07 (three-sided terminal) and GoAheadFan95's October 2025 long facade.
Their source/attribution list is outside the repo in the reference folder.

## Night

Clock panels are blank red lit rectangles (no digits), with a restrained warm
clock-shaft wash through a separate batched lmMat material. Main opaque stone
uses a local lmMat warm facade floodlight wash (`lk:.34`), falling off with
height through the existing shader; it has no blanket self-emission. The exact
exterior wash intensity is provisional as noted below. Office glazing uses
the shared office lighting. JTC's official lobby photograph
`jtc_d4d66708afe748c7a13324c5e9052180.jpg` was downloaded and visually inspected:
it confirms warm cove lighting and warm hanging bulbs in the central concourse
(the photograph is taken by day, so it does not verify exterior night washing).
Source: https://www.jtc.gov.sg/find-space/jurong-town-hall and
https://www.jtc.gov.sg/-/media/d4d66708afe748c7a13324c5e9052180.jpg .
Precise night facade wash remains provisional:
existing Commons references are all daytime. Search located a genuine dusk
photo on page 19 of URA's *Jurong Lake District Planning and Urban Design Guide*
and page 21/Figure 7 of the 2023 ULI Hines Asia Pacific competition brief, but the
PDF downloads are inaccessible (403/404). Do not claim a complete visual night
cross-check until a viewable photograph is obtained.

The 26 m body improves the primary mean from r2's 0.818 to 0.831, while the
corrected clock position now agrees with the south and close-southwest images.
This variant is retained. Residual overlays show differences along the OSM bow
corners/roof margins and a reference-only band at ground level: the footprint
mask includes a 3 m buffer, stairs, vegetation and photogrammetric column fill,
whereas the authored lower plates deliberately recess below their real eaves.
The north/east/top outlines do not support expanding the entire building to
cover those residuals. The street image is almost entirely tree occlusion.
Further indiscriminate enlargement would harm the observed cantilever and OSM
plan, so massing iteration stops here. Interior/crown detail limits below remain
honest visual approximations. Root worker performs the final combined build.

Roof plant layout, clock blade curvature and exact intermediate floor levels are
approximations. No surrounding parking lots, neighbouring structures or mature
tree canopies were added to increase the silhouette score. Landscaping stays
within the footprint. There are no subsidiary landmark keys.
