# Perennial Business City

OSM way **173029751**, eight-storey connected former Big Box warehouse/retail structure. Uses the supplied OSM anchor and complete polygon at the lower levels; the upper slab omits only the rounded southwest arrival projection. No unlabelled subkeys.

Built broad white industrial office elevations with teal ribbon windows and physical mullions, 7.5/5 m storey rhythm, olive/gold vertical perforated screen accents, lower curved planted arrival terrace, glazed entrances/canopies, blank lit sign panels, rooftop garden, solar rows, open roof frame and screened plant. All original batched geometry, no logos, image textures or external meshes.

Height **49.05 m** from the official owner's `assets/img/sectional-plan.jpg`: L1 0, L2 7.5, L3 15, L4 22.5, L5 27.5, L6 32.5, L7 37.5, L8 45, top 49.05 m. The module updates its own SGJE data height/source before registration; shared footprints.json is untouched. The owner's completed-building photos establish a broad white slab and teal lower volumes, not separate office towers. Approximate upper ribbon heights are 23.5–25.5, 28.5–30.5, 33.5–35.5 and 39–43.7 m; roof frame 48.8–49.05 m. OSM controls all main plan dimensions (~275 m east-west and ~247 m north-south overall extents).
