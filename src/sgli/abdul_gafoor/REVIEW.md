# Masjid Abdul Gafoor

OSM way 176326990, retained 39.6 × 30.2 m rotated footprint; replaces `sgabdulgafoor`.
Raised historic prayer hall with open cinquefoil verandahs, clustered pilasters,
small floral reliefs, roof balustrades, 22 parapet turrets, four corner cupolas,
25 geometric sunburst rays, a three-stage hexagonal cupola, onion domes, finials,
entry steps and the eastern flat-roof extension. No Arabic/calligraphic text;
inscription areas are blank panels. Five batched meshes, 41,479 triangles.

Model highest finial 20.07 m, prayer-hall roof 9.3 m, extension 9.35 m plus small
plant. The 21 m footprint height is provisional. Dimensions start from the old
landmark and are corrected to the real OSM footprint and the Roots restoration
photographs; pending measured references.
