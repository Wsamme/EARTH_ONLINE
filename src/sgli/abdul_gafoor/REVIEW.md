# Masjid Abdul Gafoor

OSM 176326990, retained 39.6 × 30.2 m rotated plan, replacing `sgabdulgafoor`.
Raised prayer hall with genuinely open cinquefoil verandahs, dark recessed hall
openings, clustered pilasters, geometric floral relief, balustrades, 22 parapet
turrets, four corner cupolas, 25 sunburst rays, a three-stage hexagonal cupola,
onion domes, finials, entry steps and the eastern flat-roof extension. Local +z
faces northeast toward the Dunlop Street approach (rotation 2.467 radians).
Calligraphic/inscription areas are blank panels. Green flat roofs have small plant.

Measured maximum **10.8 m**, p90 **4.3 m**, p50 **3.4 m**, from
`/workspace/li_refs/abdul_gafoor/meta.json`. Main deck is fitted to **4.3 m** and
highest finial to **10.8 m**, with the lower arcade at about 3.3 m. The initial
unmeasured sections were too tall; a piecewise vertical section fit retains the
real plan and the cupola's relative prominence. Shared data's provisional 21 m
must be regenerated by the lead; core stats still report that provisional value.
**49,799 triangles**, five shared material batches.

## Round 2 — corrected architectural frame

The top/n/e references exposed a 180-degree reversal in the inherited frame:
the green prayer hall/cupola belonged northwest, with low frontage wings southwest,
not southeast beside a full-height northwest extension. Corrected the frame to
2.467 + PI, shortened the hall/arcade depth to match the photographed roof edge,
and moved the cupola to its roof position. Frontage has five cusped bays and low
green-roofed wings, roof plant and an open court; the oversized full-height
extension is removed. These changes follow visible architecture rather than the
reference's 3 m buffer. The plinth stays on OSM 176326990.

Height rechecked against ground 14.65 m / roof max 25.42 m ellipsoidal in meta.json:
10.8 m is retained as the provisional image-fit finial height, main roof 4.3 m,
front wings about 3.5 m. The unusually low p90 4.3 m / p50 3.4 m and courtyard
sampling mean these are not a survey of each architectural element. Contemporary
NHB restoration photos determine the white/green paint and five-arch frontage.

Repeated web searches did not locate a reliable contemporary dome-visible night
photo. Retained the saved NHB/URA 1992 night image as frontage-light evidence;
green dome wash remains brief-led and explicitly unverified. Offline geometry
passes at 44,591 triangles. New exact-camera scores follow below.

r2b confirmed the cupola now lands on the photographed northwest roof, while
removing the oversized extension reduced mean IoU .698 → .678. A further genuine
missing part was visible in top/n/e: the narrow clay-roof ancillary wing on the
east side of the courtyard. Added it at 3.4 m eaves / 5.05 m ridge, with physical
tile rolls; the courtyard remains open. Corrected the sunburst/entrance to the
southeast side facing that court, rather than the southwest frontage wing. The
finial maximum remains 10.8 m. Final rerender follows this roof/entrance correction.
