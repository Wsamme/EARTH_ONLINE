# Masjid Angullia

OSM 396336447. Separate freestanding conserved three-arch gatehouse, green
balusters, cream/coral pinnacles, and the post-2020 prayer hall behind. Current
photos supersede the historical all-green gatehouse photo: the hall is coral and
cream, with pointed upper jali panels, tall blind/glazed front arches, narrow
floor bands, side windows, a small corner roof tower and roof pavilion lattice.
Source: https://www.extron.com/article/angullia . No logos or inscription text.

Hall parapet 19.9 m, highest small tower finial 25.6 m; gate pinnacles 8.57 m.
These are photo estimates, not the provisional 16 m in footprints.json; measured
reference fitting is still required. 10,000-ish triangles (see final recorded stats).

Night photo inspected and saved outside repo:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Masjid_Angullia.jpg . Warm/white upper
sconces and lit entry inform modest warm facade illumination; no green building
wash or animated lights. Historical green-gate photo is separately archived so
it cannot be mistaken for the current hall.
