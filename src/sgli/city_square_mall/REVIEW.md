# City Square Mall

OSM 102429452 retail plate and 361112097 nine-level rear part. Four retail floors with glazed bays, a curved upper atrium and barrel canopy, taller louvred service/parking block, blank signage panels, planted roof beds and a small edge eco-canopy. Adjacent City Square Residences towers are excluded. Full City Green park and its large detached structures are outside these building footprints and remain generic context.

Provisional photo/level estimates: retail parapet 22.4 m, atrium roof roughly 27.2–35.0 m, back-block roofline 41.4 m, maximum plant about 42.0 m. OSM levels=4 and 9; no measured reference available yet. The footprint height 45 m is not treated as measured evidence.

Night photo inspected: `/workspace/li_photo_refs/city_square_mall/night.jpg`, Land8 article “City Square Urban Park: Showing 2 Major Benefits of an Urban Park”, 2015 (Picture-068). Warm illuminated glass atrium and shopfronts, cooler edge accents, predominantly dark solid walls. Day façade from CDL 2017 BCA award press photograph. Both external image sources are logged beside the images. The latest 2026 refurbishment is not claimed to be reproduced.

Known gaps: detached park canopy is represented only at the building edge, no full park footprint is supplied; atrium roof/massing awaits matched references. Pending IoU and runtime audit.
