# Deepavali light-up — round 2

Original batched ribbon geometry interprets the 2024 LISHA paired-peacock gateway, lotus/diya/flower panels, and the dense magenta/gold floral scrolls visible in 2024–2025 photographs. No letters, logos, photographic textures or reference meshes ship. The lead's `noHull` integration is preserved, so shophouses remain visible beneath the street installation.

Night photographs inspected: `/workspace/li_photo_refs/deepavali_lightup/night2024.jpg`, Outlook Traveller 26 October 2024, WS Streetlights Deepavali2024-17 (https://www.outlooktraveller.com/destinations/international/heres-what-to-do-in-singapores-little-india-this-diwali-2024); `night2025.jpg`, Tabla's 2025 Serangoon Road/LISHA report (https://www.tabla.com.sg/community/events/serangoon-road-light-sees-utsavam-return). Source URLs and original image URLs are recorded in the external SOURCES.md. Both publication pages were rechecked in round 2. Day checks use Merlion Channel's unlit lamp/lotus photo `day.jpg`.

Changes: ribbon widths increase from 5.5 to 9.5 cm, emissive gain rises from 0.95 to 2.8, saturated gold/magenta/orange/green/violet colours remain vertex-coloured through the night shader, and repeating panels gain floral scrolls, side lotuses and extra diyas. The gentle twinkle remains 0.96 ± 0.04 at 0.65 rad/s (period 9.67 s), with spatial phase variation; it has no on/off flashes. `lmMat` gates all emission with the shared night uniform. Side motifs turn inward to stay within the support span. Geometry is **59,688 triangles in two batches**, below 80k.

Placement and spacing: 33 Serangoon spans follow ordered OSM centrelines and sample terrain individually; 32.00 m nominal centreline arc spacing is measured from the model sampler, not a surveyed installation interval. Source photographs establish approximate spacing and the hierarchy of a taller arrival gateway over repeated smaller spans; a single uncalibrated perspective photo cannot prove exact physical metres. One candidate span (sample index 3) is omitted to clear a mapped frontage; its neighbouring spans have a 64 m sampling gap, with no unsupported garland crossing that gap. Nine other corridor spans narrow as required, giving 8–12 m clear support spans. Repeating supports are 8.3 m high, central motif clearance about 7.1 m, gateway crown **11.89 m**. Side ornaments are lower than the central vehicle passage. Heights are photographic estimates, not tile measurements. Campbell Lane retains stalls and strings every 12 m; Dunlop has modest lamp strings.

Clearance audit now checks **2,771** available OSM buildings (the full district extract `/workspace/li_data/map.osm` plus the two detailed context extracts). All **66** Serangoon support centres clear those footprints; the separate module-footprint audit also reports zero collisions and no invalid coordinates. OSM coverage and the point-support audit do not establish surveyed utilities, lane clearances or a complete structural engineering check.

No IoU applies. Street-level day/night proof is written to `/workspace/eo_li_out/commerce/deepavali_lightup_review_street_night.jpg` by `tools/li_commerce_review.mjs`, using a centreline eye 2.2 m above the road. The round-2 model is substantially brighter and denser than round 1; it still simplifies the much denser real gateway artwork, and does not reproduce any one year's exact installation plan. Festival stall scheduling and exact utility/installation positions remain unknown.

Final street-night render inspected after the clearance corrections: gateway and repeating panels are visibly saturated and bright, with shophouses preserved by `noHull`. Generic tree foliage still occludes the far-left edge of the arrival motif; this has not been hidden for the proof. Final full-app statistics confirm 59,688 triangles, zero pageErrors and zero consoleErrors. The centreline camera uses local sampled road ground, rather than the district anchor's ground.

Inspected the full Serangoon Road contact sheet and the local September 2024 night panorama `017_R.jpg` directly: white/cool-violet crossed floral loops, gold lamp centres, violet rosettes and bright shop bands are visible. Perpendicular, often cropped night panoramas cannot independently calibrate those metre values, so no false precision in spacing improvement is claimed. The centreline terrain sampling and noHull remain. Entire-year installation layout, full sculptural artwork, exact spacing and utility positions remain approximate; night refs have no skyline IoU score. All references remain external and were never fetched again or shipped.
