# Deepavali light-up

Original batched ribbon outlines inspired by the 2024 LISHA peacock gateway and repeating lotus/diya/flower panels. Serangoon Road centrelines are joined by their ordered OSM vertices, limited to the Rochor–Kitchener corridor (local south 297 m to north -600 m), with 32 m repeated spans, 12 m nominal width (spans 20 and 21 narrowed to 10 m and 8 m to clear mapped building corners), lowest motif around 7.1 m above the terrain, and one taller peacock gateway. Narrow longitudinal garlands follow road bends. Campbell Lane has small festival stalls and 12 m overhead strings; Dunlop has modest lamp strings. Terrain is sampled at every installation rather than using one flat district base.

Height source: photographic proportions only; no building IoU applies. Repeating support top 8.3 m, gateway maximum including ribbon edges 11.89 m, Campbell strings 5.4 m. These are estimates, not a survey. Restrained 4% sine modulation at 0.65 rad/s keeps a gentle twinkle, gated by the shared lmMat night uniform; daylight wires stay visible and unlit.

Night reference inspected: `/workspace/li_photo_refs/deepavali_lightup/night2024.jpg`, Outlook Traveller 26 October 2024, WS Streetlights Deepavali 2024-17 (paired peacocks, golden arch, magenta, gold, green, orange and violet lamp/lotus panels). Sources are outside the repository. The composition is an interpretation of the 2024 edition, not a claim of a current year layout. Text omitted.

No IoU. Geometry audit: 41,692 triangles in two batches; 34 Serangoon spans, zero support poles inside any supplied landmark building footprint. A second independent check uses 330 ordinary building polygons from the Arcade/Mustafa OSM context XMLs; after narrowing those two spans, all 68 poles clear the tested buildings. Coverage is limited to these two extracts, not the entire corridor. Day appearance was checked against Merlion Channel’s unlit lamp/lotus street photograph; the 2025 Tabla street view was also downloaded for spacing context.
