# Little India shophouse rows — Street View pass

Original geometry from the supplied OSM per-unit footprints, with the street-facing edge chosen by closest segment to `front.point`. References stay external; no imagery, textures, logos, text or external meshes ship. The nearest named street is a facade heuristic, not a surveyed entrance designation.

Inspected all five local street contact sheets first. June 2024 Campbell Lane and March 2024 Dunlop Street establish predominantly two-storey plaster rows, tall paired/tripled shutters, pale raised trims, terracotta roofs, recessed ground shops, continuous sign bands and a palette of ochre, mint, rose, blue, cream and violet. February 2024 Buffalo Road supports stronger ochre/blue accents. March 2024 Race Course Road includes both two/three-storey rows and modern sites/construction, not a continuous heritage frontage. September 2024 Serangoon images are night references; June/February 2024 day images supply permanent paint. Older 2018–2019 views are subordinate where newer evidence exists. Paint is deterministic by OSM id, sampled as a district palette rather than claimed parcel-by-parcel colour matching.

Built 1,299 units, three batches, **241,008 triangles**. Street-side lower walls are clipped back **1.8 m** for a real five-foot way; columns, upper pilasters, cornices, recessed shop glazing, blank sign panels, paired/tripled upper shutters with centre stiles and five louvre strokes, pitched terracotta roofs or parapets use compact batched geometry. Rear footprints remain exact OSM polygons. Ground is sampled with `G.at` independently at each unit centroid; no district-wide flat base. Upper glazing is sparsely warm-lit; shops and blank sign bands are warm, following September 2024 Serangoon and September 2019 Dunlop night imagery. No extra Street View was fetched.

Heights: OSM `building:levels` takes precedence, positive `height` where available; zero heights are treated as unknown. Otherwise predominantly two storeys, with a deterministic minority of three-storey units on Serangoon/Race Course streets based on sampled rhythm. Ground storey **3.9 m**, upper storeys **3.3 m**. Wall heights **3.9–13.8 m** including supplied one/four-storey tags; pitched roofs add at most 1.8 m. No tile references/meta.json exist for this new district module at implementation time; no tile IoU is claimed.

Extract contamination is handled locally before registration, keeping shared footprints untouched. Twelve modern/large footprints remain in the ordinary base-city pass rather than being replaced with invented shophouses: **121874708, 172999757, 176326991, 184914354–184914358, 191009978, 223871622, 1078263457, 1429719916**. The two HDB slab ids **178436578 and 178436580** retain skip masks but are rendered only by `tekka_centre`, preventing duplicate low-rise replacements. 178436579 is also guarded as Tekka context, though absent from this extract. Thus 1,313 supplied parts = 1,299 new units + 12 base-city exceptions + 2 existing Tekka slabs. The lead should refine the shophouse extraction to exclude these non-shophouse sites upstream.

Known gaps: no individual architectural survey of all 1,299 parcels; most secondary streets have no supplied Street View samples. Window spacing and roof/colour variants are a conservative district grammar. Rear services, individual sculpted plaster relief and operable shutters are simplified; louvres use five broad strokes for LOD. Point terrain samples do not follow slopes across each entire footprint. Large modern exceptions retain the existing generic city's limitations. Whole-scene skyline IoU includes trees, neighbouring buildings and terrain, and cannot isolate facade improvement.
