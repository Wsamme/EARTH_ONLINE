# Sri Srinivasa Perumal Temple

Replaces `srinivasa`. OSM 481614952 is the gopuram at the eastern edge of the
site (centre +53.49 m east, -0.84 m south from the compound anchor), verified
against the older landmark anchor and public photographs. The four other mapped
parts 454182405 / 454182408 / 454182454 / 455155906 are separately modelled halls
with pilasters, shaded arched verandahs, roof-edge miniature shrines and sanctums.
No geometry uses the oversized landuse way 686747406.

Five tapering gopuram tiers, geometric painted deity bands, sala crown with seven
finials, guardian figures, timber entrance, compound wall wings and flagstaff.
Gopuram maximum 20 m uses the public documented approximate tower height;
hall eaves 6.8–7.9 m and shrine heights 11.6–14.1 m are photo estimates pending
measured refs. Footprints' 21 m is provisional. Official history:
https://heb.org.sg/sspt/ (1966 hall, 1979 tower; no date text on model).
