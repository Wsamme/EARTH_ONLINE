# House of Tan Teng Niah

OSM 178436852, replacing `sgtantengniah`. Two-storey villa on its actual stepped
plan, recessed red entrance under the five-foot way, blue/yellow front columns,
paired multicolour louvred shutters, round lunettes and geometric carved/floral
panels, fluted pilasters, coloured fascia boards, bracket dentils, timber door
panels, intersecting terracotta hip roofs, shallow green canopy and small rear
air conditioners. Inscription boards are blank. No logos or photographic textures.

Measured maximum **11.5 m**, p90 **10.2 m**, p50 **8.6 m**, from
`/workspace/li_refs/tan_teng_niah/meta.json`. Main ridge is fitted to **11.5 m**;
eaves remain 7.8 m and upper floor 3.85 m, preserving the photographed window
proportions. **23,909 triangles**, three shared material batches. The shared
11 m metadata is provisional and should be regenerated by the lead.

## Validation and limits

Offline finite geometry and build syntax checks pass. Earlier full-app/detail
runs had zero page and console errors and no retired placements. The final
measured-camera pass follows the 11.5 m roof fit. Fine historic carving is
abstracted as geometry. Roof curvature is approximated by hip surfaces with
physical narrow tile rolls. The older tile photos show a low front forecourt
structure not present in the 2023 night photo; it has not been added to the
current doorway. Nearby temporary stalls and tents are omitted.

The final roof has equal-pitch hips converging to short ridges determined by its
real plan, with physical tile rolls on all four faces. Main-view mean improved
from r8's 0.693 to r9's 0.704 after shortening the overly long ridges, and remains
0.704 in r10 with equal-pitch hips. Remaining differences include the 3 m buffer,
the obsolete front forecourt structure and adjacent-building/tree contamination;
no mass was added to fill them.
