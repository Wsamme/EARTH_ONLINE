# House of Tan Teng Niah

OSM 178436852, replaces `sgtantengniah`. Two-storey villa on its actual stepped
plan, recessed red entry under the five-foot way, blue/yellow front columns,
paired multicolour louvred shutters, lunettes and geometric floral/carved panels,
fluted pilasters, multicolour fascia boards, bracket dentils, timber door panels,
intersecting tiled hip roofs, shallow green canopy and small rear air conditioners.
Maximum 10.915 m (roof ridge), upper eaves 7.8 m, floor 3.85 m: photographic
proportions using STB and NHB photos; 11 m in footprint data is provisional.
